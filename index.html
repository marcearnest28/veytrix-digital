ABOUT ME

I'm Marc Earnest.

Builder • Problem Solver • Developer

I come from a hands-on paving and construction background, where good tools have to work in the real world — not just look good on a screen.

I got into software because I kept seeing problems that could be solved better with the right app. That led me to build AsphaltPro, my first Android app, around the kind of information and tools paving crews actually use.

Since then, I’ve been learning and building more — from Android apps to websites and custom business tools. I like taking an idea, figuring out how it should work, and turning it into something useful, clean, and practical.
